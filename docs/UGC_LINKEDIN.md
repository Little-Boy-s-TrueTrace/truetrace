# LinkedIn Post -- TrueTrace by Little Boy's

What if AI could stop financial crime before it even happens?

For the past few days, our team has been running on coffee and adrenaline at the #QoderHackathon. We are incredibly excited to unveil our project: **TrueTrace** -- a Multi-Agent AI Compliance Platform built to revolutionize how banks handle AML and KYC.

Instead of relying on manual reviews and scattered data, TrueTrace deploys a specialized trio of AI agents:

**Deepfake Inspector**: Instantly spots manipulated IDs and synthetic media during onboarding.
**Money Trail Explorer**: Tracks complex transaction webs to identify laundering patterns in real-time.
**STR Report Generator**: Automatically drafts bilingual Suspicious Transaction Reports to save compliance officers hours of tedious paperwork.

Building this complex microservices architecture in a short time was a massive challenge. But leveraging Qoder's AI-assisted development tools allowed us to iterate at lightning speed and bring our vision to life much faster than we thought possible.

We wired it all together using a robust tech stack: Spring Boot, Python, React, Kafka, and the powerful capabilities of @Alibaba Cloud (Qwen).

We cannot wait to pitch this to the judges. The hacker energy here has been absolutely incredible!
